# CH05 — The Shipment

Time: 20 October, evening to midnight. Canonical authority: [STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Opening situation

A refrigerated convoy carries Elena, coerced technicians and detainee credential hardware toward an offshore transfer vessel.

## Major events

The pair enter a bonded warehouse through maintenance access, identify occupied containers using ventilation loads, and redirect a lifting gantry to isolate Elena’s carriage. Noah wants to extract her immediately; Elena insists on opening the other occupied containers. Adrian and Noah operate separate controls while Elena reroutes a transmitter. They escape with detainees on a tug organized by dock allies, not a magically waiting getaway craft.

## Character development

Noah listens when Elena tells him what rescue requires. Adrian apologizes without asking for forgiveness. Elena takes an active engineering role.

## Conflict

Removing the wrong refrigeration module could kill captives; occupancy and power labels must be verified.

## Important discoveries

The shipment keys open a subterranean continuity facility and contain checksums that can prove the ledger unaltered.

## Plot twists

Elena maintained part of the system to keep captives alive and embedded a disclosure path inside its safety channel.

## Chapter climax

Both protagonists keep the gantry stable while Elena walks the last detainee across; the convoy alarm forces a water exit.

## Ending transition

Elena identifies a land access shaft to the underground command archive. Mara receives the rescued patients.

## Connection to next chapter

CH06 must authenticate the command record before Voss erases the ledger and blames a rogue contractor.
