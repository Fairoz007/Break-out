# CH03 — Other Shore

Time: 19 October, predawn to afternoon. Canonical authority: [STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Opening situation

Fugitives have reached the tidal shelf with a damaged radio and no safe road route.

## Major events

They cross sea caves during a low-tide window, repair an abandoned survey skiff, and evade harbor contractor searches. Noah contacts a dock colleague without disclosing Mara’s location. A relay cache holds an encrypted message using Elena’s childhood phrase, current date and a maintenance fault Noah can independently verify. Adrian recognizes the relay address from his failed verification query.

## Character development

Hope makes Noah reckless; Adrian delays a confession he now suspects he must make.

## Conflict

They must fuel the skiff without revealing the dock community to a search sweep.

## Important discoveries

Elena is alive and held on a moving contractor supply chain. The message requests an archive warrant index, not an immediate armed rescue.

## Plot twists

Noah’s old cargo contained biometric keys for detainee manifests, not medical supplies. He admits knowingly taking illegal cargo while rejecting responsibility for crimes he did not know about.

## Chapter climax

The skiff clears the search perimeter after both players release an entangled mooring and protect the relay cache.

## Ending transition

The archive index points to an urban records annex. They change clothes with practical help and enter the city separately.

The indexed contracts and relay query history are available in CH04’s city archive.
