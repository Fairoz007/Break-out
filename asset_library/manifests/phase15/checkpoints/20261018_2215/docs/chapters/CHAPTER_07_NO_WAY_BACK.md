# CH07 — No Way Back

Time: 21 October, dawn. Canonical authority: [STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Opening situation

Storm damage has split access to the emergency array. Elena carries the disclosure package while Voss’s team closes in.

## Major events

Adrian and Noah reach separate gantries, align the array and establish an independent receiving station through Mara’s journalist contact. Elena removes detainee identities before publication. Preserved command signatures and shipment checksums allow authenticated broadcast; without them, testimony and partial documents transmit but cannot clear Adrian or dismantle VANTAGE immediately. A structural failure separates the protagonists during the evacuation. Each player receives an explicit rescue-or-leave choice, with time to understand the consequence.

## Character development

Trust is demonstrated through rescue after all secrets are known. Forgiveness remains a personal response, not a reward for selecting the correct button.

## Conflict

The array must transmit before shutdown while both people remain reachable.

## Important discoveries

The institution can be exposed without publishing the private identities it exploited.

## Plot twists

No scripted surprise makes either protagonist a secret VANTAGE mastermind. The final uncertainty is what they will do for one another.

## Chapter climax

Both assisting creates a reciprocal escape through the service cradle. One abandoning leaves the assisting partner exposed and killed while the abandoning partner escapes. Both abandoning routes fail as isolated gantries collapse; both die. No input defaults to assist after a clearly stated accessibility-adjustable decision window.

## Ending transition

Survivors meet Elena and Mara at a civilian rescue launch. The broadcast outcome and survival outcome are shown separately in grounded epilogues.
