# CH06 — Truth Below

Time: 21 October, 02:00–05:30. Canonical authority: [STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Opening situation

The protagonists and Elena enter a coastal underground archive while Mara moves survivors to independent medical care.

## Major events

They traverse decommissioned service galleries, compare hardware seals to the shipment checksums, and recover Vale’s murder authorization with Voss’s command signature. Rusk arrives with a clinic transfer log and a recorded coercion order; Iris is already being moved to Mara’s care by an adult ally. A lockdown threatens remaining detainees. Players choose whether to preserve an independent evidence mirror or divert its only available power channel to an evacuation lift; both routes still save some witnesses, but only the mirror route retains a complete authentication chain.

## Character development

Adrian accepts that legal vindication may be lost to save people. Noah must weigh other families as seriously as his own.

## Conflict

There is no clean action that guarantees every person and every record survives.

## Important discoveries

VANTAGE began as disaster logistics and deliberately expanded into profitable off-record detention; it is an organization of accountable people, not an inexplicable omnipotent force.

## Plot twists

Rusk testifies to his own crime rather than offering a convenient secret that absolves it.

## Chapter climax

Voss destroys local storage, but the pair carry a protected capsule and Elena retains a redacted publication plan.

## Ending transition

The only transmitter outside contractor control is the old coastal emergency array; pursuit drives them above ground.

## Connection to next chapter

CH07 resolves evidence verification and the partners’ survival through player actions.
