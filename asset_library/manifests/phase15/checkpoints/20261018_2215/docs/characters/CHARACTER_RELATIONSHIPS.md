## Information discipline

Before CH03 neither player can assert Elena is alive. CH01 reveals a VANTAGE transport seal, prisoner-number notation and cached voice fragment only; a recent cache-copy date cannot authenticate when the voice was recorded. CH02 Mara reports a living unidentified engineer; CH03 Elena’s private childhood phrase and current timestamp authenticate the recording. CH04 supplies the relay-query log and Adrian’s confession. CH05 reunites the siblings. CH06 proves Vale’s murder order and ties command signatures to the ledger. CH07 publishes only redacted verification records; detainee biometric keys are never broadcast.

## Dialogue decision IDs

`DEC_CH01_COVER`: Noah’s answer to Adrian’s inquiry (guarded or candid), converges before workshop access. `DEC_CH04_CONFESSION`: players choose delivery and response, never whether the canonical confession happens. `DEC_CH06_EVIDENCE`: preserve independent ledger mirror or sacrifice it to open the detainee evacuation channel; remaining authentication can be lost. `DEC_CH07_RESCUE`: each player independently chooses assist or leave during the final timed separation; these choices determine which protagonist survives. No dialogue choice deletes a necessary clue.
