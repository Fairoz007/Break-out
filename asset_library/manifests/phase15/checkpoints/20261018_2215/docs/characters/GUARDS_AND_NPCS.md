# Guards and supporting cast

Canonical biographies. Names are fiction; casting and appearance are production specifications, not acquired assets.
## Tomas Rusk — `CHAR_RUSK`, 41

**Background:** Blackridge senior guard; daughter Iris, nine, receives life-preserving treatment from a VANTAGE-controlled clinic.

**Personality:** Gentle in private, brittle under supervision.

**Appearance:** Heavy build, receding hair, left-handed, wedding band on chain; worn guard uniform.

**Motivation:** Keep Iris alive.

**Fear:** Her treatment stopping because he disobeyed.

**Strength:** Knows shift routines and service locks.

**Weakness:** Rationalizes each coercive act as the last.

**Relationships:** Mara knows the clinic leverage but not the planned ambush.

**Secret:** Reports the escape route after being threatened with a treatment cutoff.

**Development:** His CH01 betrayal remains his responsibility; CH02 he supplies a transfer time, then CH06 gives testimony instead of seeking absolution.

**Outcome:** Survives in protective custody if evidence is preserved; otherwise flees with Iris and remains vulnerable.

## Dr Mara Sen — `CHAR_MARA`, 46

**Background:** Prison physician who documented unexplained inmate transfers and concealed copies of intake injuries.

**Personality:** Calm, direct, impatient with heroic gestures.

**Appearance:** 167 cm, Indian heritage, short silver-streaked hair, practical scrubs and cardigan.

**Motivation:** Get patients out and preserve their consent.

**Fear:** Her records becoming a list for retaliation.

**Strength:** Triage, patient trust, careful documentation.

**Weakness:** Exhaustion and limited access.

**Relationships:** Helps both protagonists; communicates with Elena via coded telemetry.

**Secret:** Has falsified transfer classifications to keep several prisoners alive.

**Development:** Moves from quiet harm reduction to public testimony.

**Outcome:** Leaves with rescued detainees in CH06; testifies in evidence-preserved endings.

## Silas Bell — `CHAR_SILAS`, 38

**Background:** Laundry orderly and yard broker serving an extortion sentence.

**Personality:** Performatively cheerful, pragmatic, never omniscient.

**Appearance:** 180 cm, shaved head, broad hands, chipped incisor, patched prison uniform.

**Motivation:** Buy a safe future without owning anyone else.

**Fear:** Being the disposable middleman.

**Strength:** Schedules, relationships, practical logistics.

**Weakness:** Charges for help and initially avoids personal risk.

**Relationships:** Uses Noah’s confrontation to test both newcomers, then offers workshop access.

**Secret:** He sells ordinary favors, not the escape route; the betrayal is Rusk’s.

**Development:** Chooses to move vulnerable inmates from a dangerous corridor without payment.

**Outcome:** Remains in Blackridge until an investigation opens; never magically escapes offscreen.

## Celia Voss — `CHAR_VOSS`, 52

**Background:** Former logistics commander, executive director of VANTAGE’s clandestine continuity program.

**Personality:** Controlled, managerial, convinced legality follows successful operations.

**Appearance:** 176 cm, close-cropped greying hair, severe civilian suit or weatherproof field coat.

**Motivation:** Protect an extractive detention system and her authority.

**Fear:** A record she cannot discredit or buy.

**Strength:** Institutional manipulation, logistics, strategic patience.

**Weakness:** Treats people as predictable liabilities.

**Relationships:** Commands Blackridge contractor liaison; sees Elena as infrastructure.

**Secret:** Ordered Vale’s murder and fabrication of Adrian’s access trail.

**Development:** Increasingly abandons plausible deniability as her system fails.

**Outcome:** Exposed and detained if authenticated evidence broadcasts; otherwise escapes investigation, wounded institutionally but still dangerous.

## Daniel Vale — `CHAR_VALE`, 57

**Background:** Minister of public infrastructure who initially signed emergency contracts before discovering forced transfers.

**Personality:** Measured, politically compromised, belatedly courageous.

**Appearance:** Grey hair, glasses, formal clothes; appears only in archival material.

**Motivation:** Expose the program he helped fund.

**Fear:** His confession being reduced to partisan scandal.

**Strength:** Access to signed contracts and oversight seals.

**Weakness:** Delayed action to protect his career.

**Relationships:** Recruited Adrian and received Elena’s attempted disclosure.

**Secret:** Approved the original opaque contracting framework without knowing its eventual detention use.

**Development:** His records reveal complicity and corrective action together.

**Outcome:** Killed eleven weeks before CH01; no resurrection or living double.

## Population roles

`CHAR_GUARD`: shift guards share patrol rules, never teleport between zones. `CHAR_INMATE`: workshop workers and background population require distinct silhouette/outfit variations. Silas personally provokes Noah over the approved photograph, then retreats after guard intervention; he is not secretly VANTAGE leadership. Iris Rusk is an offscreen child referenced only by a sealed clinic envelope; no child model is required for CH01. Voss, Vale and Elena have no physical CH01 appearance. Uniform liaison badge carries `PROP_VANTAGE_BADGE`; generic prison guards do not all wear it.
