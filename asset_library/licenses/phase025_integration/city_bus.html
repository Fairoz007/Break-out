Description

Low-floor city bus. Overall 3.2 x 12 x 3.1 m. Key dimensions: body length 11800 mm; body width 2500 mm; wheelbase 6750 mm; body roof height 2900 mm. Original two-axle urban bus exterior with two curbside passenger doors, route display, glazing, roof air conditioner and editable wheel assemblies. Nominal architectural scale; interior seating, powertrain and working door mechanisms are omitted. glTF binary (GLB) mesh with materials, modeled in meters at real-world scale.
