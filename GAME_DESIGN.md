# BREAKOUT: NO WAY BACK — game design

Phase01 pre-production only. Godot4.x stable, GDScript, Windows PC, realistic3D/PBR third-person cooperative thriller. Two players: P1 Adrian Cross, P2 Noah Reyes. Initial local vertical split-screen; online co-op is future work. [STORY.md](STORY.md) is canonical for narrative; screenplay governs exact performance and [missions](docs/missions/CH01_MISSION_01.md) govern production gates.

Chapter1 target36minutes inclusive of6minutes cinematics: New Blood330s, Trouble in the Yard360s, Workshop420s, Lights Out360s, Behind the Walls420s, Betrayal270s. Later six chapters are treatments, not production scripts. Narrative clock gaps use explicit chapter scene transitions; estimated play time is not literal prison clock time.

Design pillars: interdependent observation and action; grounded environments with clear spatial logic; trust built through small reciprocal risks; cinematic emotional consequence. Neither player is permanent leader or helper. Adrian reads structure and manages deliberate actions; Noah spots human behavior and carries practical risk. Ownership alternates across missions. No stat bonuses encode personality.

Core loop planned: observe route and partner clue→share information→ready distinct interactions→perform safe coordinated action→react to dialogue consequence→commit shared checkpoint. Chapter1 contains parallel observation, social distraction, safe machine interlock, coordinated stealth, fictional relay gate and authored capture. No combat implementation, lethal electrical handling, real lock instruction or open-world simulation is required. Capture is intentional story transition, clearly presented, not a surprise failed objective.

Player movement proposed walking/crouching/interacting, contextual ladders/hatches, constrained camera look. No jump puzzle, stamina or weapon inventory required for Chapter1. Interaction prompts identify actor role and partner waiting state; hold actions may be toggled for accessibility. Guard detection provides warning and deterministic retry at CP04_A. Puzzle errors reset safely without killing partner. Waiting for partner never incurs an arbitrary deadline.

Presentation: realistic adult proportions, worn concrete/steel, restrained prison palette, cold daylight and practical night pools. Distinct faces/clothing silhouettes remain legible at half-screen width. Shared full-screen cinematics require both ready and mutual skip. Subtitles identify speaker and sound source; adjustable text, visual audio cues, shape-coded puzzle states, reduced strobe and sensitivity/invert options are required future acceptance items. Full script contains every spoken line; no invented gameplay barks at implementation time.

World scope: intake+cellblock, yard, laundry workshop, maintenance network including spillway interception and solitary annex. Exact metric blockout proposals live under docs/environments; test all camera and two-body door clearances before detailed art. Asset requirements are not proof of acquisition; refer [manifest](asset_library/manifests/assets.json), [gaps](docs/assets/ASSET_GAPS.md), [validation](docs/assets/ASSET_VALIDATION_REPORT.md). Realistic characters/custom contacts remain mandatory production dependencies.

Success measures for next phase: both players contribute to each gate; no lost critical dialogue; route and retry clear without external guide;36minute target tested with new players; readable half-screen UI; no camera intersection or machine contact clipping; stable checkpoint restore. Performance budgets await named minimum PC and pinned engine. Out of scope now: gameplay scripts, finished levels, final voices, combat, online networking and claimed shipped assets.
