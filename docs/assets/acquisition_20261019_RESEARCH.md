No authenticated library was accessed in this research; do not claim specific missing clips exist or were acquired. Keep raw Mixamo assets within the development team and do not publish a raw asset bundle as the deliverable.

Prioritize turn-in-place, pull, door open/close, restrained walk, controlled kneel/surrender and hand gestures after checking current Rocketbox action inventory. Existing Bip01/UAL clips are not automatically compatible with Mixamo skeletons: retarget, check feet/root motion, hand contacts and facial channels on each hero. Assisted climb/help-up, forearm stopping Noah, guard cuffing, dual handle pull, press lever/screen, hatch hold and handset/selector/brake actions need authored alignment and synchronized takes. No generic mocap download proves those contacts.
