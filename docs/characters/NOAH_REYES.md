# Noah Reyes

Canonical ID: `CHAR_NOAH`. Age: 29. Narrative authority: [STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Background

Dock mechanic and informal boat courier from the eastern port district. Took a lucrative medical refrigeration run to pay Elena’s mounting debts. The refrigerated modules carried encrypted detainee biometric keys, not medicine. He was arrested on genuine smuggling charges embellished into violent trafficking.

## Personality

Quick, observant, deflects fear with humor; warmth becomes anger when someone lies about family.

## Appearance and outfit

178 cm; wiry shoulders, warm brown skin, wavy black cropped hair, small burn on right wrist. Blue prison undershirt, orange trousers, repaired boots. Elena’s photograph is briefly confiscated at intake, then returned as approved paper effects and kept inside his shirt.

## Motivation

Find Elena and stop treating rescue as something he must accomplish alone.

## Fears

That he enabled Elena’s disappearance and cannot repair it.

## Strengths

Machine repair, improvisation, social reading and courage under pressure.

## Weaknesses

Acts before checking consequences; loyalty can make him ignore other captives.

## Relationships

Elena is his older sister. Adrian becomes an uneasy partner then a chosen brother; forgiveness is possible but never demanded.

## Secrets

He knew his cargo was illegal and lied about an entirely innocent delivery. He did not know what the keys unlocked.

## Development

From family-only loyalty toward responsibility for everyone harmed by the shipment.

## Final outcome

Can reunite with Elena if the shipment rescue succeeds; that rescue is mandatory to progress, though Elena may be injured. His survival depends on the final mutual-rescue decision.
