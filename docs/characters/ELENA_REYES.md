# Elena Reyes

Canonical ID: `CHAR_ELENA`. Age: 32. Narrative authority: [STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Background

Signals engineer hired to maintain maritime emergency relays. Discovered those relays routed VANTAGE prisoner transfers. Detained after attempting to send Vale a ledger checksum. A staged harbor accident concealed her disappearance.

## Personality

Precise, stubborn, compassionate without sentimentality; exhaustion has sharpened her humor.

## Appearance and outfit

172 cm; brown skin, dark hair roughly tied back, abrasion scars at restraint points, industrial work clothes and hearing protection. No glamorized captivity.

## Motivation

Free detainees and expose the system without releasing their private biometric identities.

## Fears

That Noah will risk himself for the version of her he remembers rather than listen to her now.

## Strengths

Radio engineering, encryption operations, quiet coalition building.

## Weaknesses

Overworks and assumes she must solve technical problems herself.

## Relationships

Protective older sister who must accept Noah as an adult. Collaborates with Mara through an indirect medical telemetry channel.

## Secrets

Has kept a deliberately incomplete relay alive to protect other captives; apparent cooperation is survival work, not a secret villain allegiance.

## Development

Reclaims authority over the evidence and chooses publication safeguards herself.

## Final outcome

Escapes CH07 with survivors if rescue is prioritized. Evidence destruction does not erase her testimony, but limits what it can prove.
