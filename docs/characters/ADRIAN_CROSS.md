# Adrian Cross

Canonical ID: `CHAR_ADRIAN`. Age: 34. Narrative authority: [STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Background

Former civilian financial investigator seconded to parliamentary procurement oversight. He found VANTAGE invoices hidden in emergency transport contracts. Minister Daniel Vale asked him to preserve a rendition ledger; Vale was killed before the handoff and Adrian was framed using manipulated access footage.

## Personality

Methodical, restrained, dryly funny when he feels safe. His stillness can read as contempt.

## Appearance and outfit

184 cm; lean, tired eyes, short dark hair with a grey temple, healed left eyebrow cut. Faded grey prison shirt, orange overshirt, worn boots; no tactical costume.

## Motivation

Clear his name by making the evidence independently verifiable, then learn to value a living witness above his own vindication.

## Fears

That his need to prove the truth will get another source killed.

## Strengths

Pattern recognition, patient observation, careful mechanical hands.

## Weaknesses

Controls information and mistakes silence for protection.

## Relationships

Initially treats Noah as useful access. Learns to trust his judgment and ultimately offers him the unedited record of his own mistake.

## Secrets

He transmitted an unredacted verification query through Elena’s relay before his arrest. It revealed her location to VANTAGE. He did not know her identity then; he suspects the link in CH03 and confesses in CH04.

## Development

From solitary investigator to accountable partner. He must tell Noah what happened even if Noah refuses forgiveness.

## Final outcome

Survives only if the player rescue condition is met. A preserved evidence chain can clear him; survival without that chain leaves him publicly accused.
