# CH02 — Night Shift

Time: 19 October, 01:10–04:40. Canonical authority: [STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Opening situation

Separated in solitary, both have bruises and limited knowledge of the other’s condition. Mara demands examination under a signed injury protocol.

## Major events

Mara tells each man exactly what she can arrange: a supervised infirmary transfer, temporary restraints and one service elevator window. Rusk supplies a real transfer time and the missing restraint key, accepting that they may expose him. Adrian distracts an escort through a disputed medication record while Noah opens a service panel with Mara’s authorized tool. They rendezvous in linen storage, move two ill detainees away from the exhaust plant, and exit through a disused drainage survey hatch.

## Character development

Noah tests Rusk’s information instead of trusting his apology. Adrian shares the whole route with Noah.

## Conflict

A contractor audit accelerates transport schedules; escape must not leave patients in danger.

## Important discoveries

Mara has medical telemetry suggesting a living engineer maintained the contractor relay after Elena’s reported death; identity is unconfirmed.

## Plot twists

Rusk’s help is genuine but cannot undo his betrayal. Mara refuses to flee until her patients have transport.

## Chapter climax

The pair open the survey hatch from opposite sides as a search team closes on the infirmary.

## Ending transition

They reach a tidal service shelf beneath Blackridge; a closing storm blocks the main road.

## Connection to next chapter

The hatch empties onto the coast, requiring boat transport and an independent check of Mara’s telemetry.
