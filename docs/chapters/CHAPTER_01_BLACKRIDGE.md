# CH01 — Blackridge

Time: 18 October 2028, dawn to night; 36-minute first-play target. Canonical authority: [STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Opening situation

Noah arrives at the coastal prison believing Elena died in a harbor accident. Adrian has spent eleven weeks inside after being framed for Vale’s murder.

## Major events

M01 New Blood (5.5 min): intake confiscation and approved return of Elena’s photograph, Adrian observes a contractor’s VANTAGE seal, Rusk assigns adjacent cells. M02 Trouble in the Yard (6 min): Silas’s provocation forces Adrian to intervene; Noah creates a nonviolent distraction and both are marched to work. M03 The Workshop (7 min): Silas’s assignment access and Mara’s medical textiles audit place them near a service diagram; Adrian holds the idle press isolation lever while Noah clears its screen; Noah then holds the inspection lamp while Adrian reads the diagram. M04 Lights Out (6 min): the pair coordinate sight lines through a restricted corridor during a documented patrol gap. M05 Behind the Walls (7 min): separate maintenance routes require a fictional A/B/C selector operated by Adrian while Noah holds the mechanical brake; Adrian selects B, then reunion at the final service door. M06 The Betrayal (4.5 min): the alarm sounds as Rusk arrives with a security team; his planted route is exposed, with a sealed clinic envelope having foreshadowed external leverage. Both are captured and separated into solitary.

## Character development

Adrian first chooses a person over remaining invisible. Noah offers practical trust before personal disclosure. Neither has earned complete confidence.

## Conflict

The route is physically viable but institutionally compromised: someone with access knows their timing.

## Important discoveries

VANTAGE operates through prison transport administration. A numbered transfer notation links the contractor seal to the same contract family Adrian investigated; it does not establish Elena’s survival.

## Plot twists

A sealed clinic envelope and Rusk’s visible fear seed coercion before the ambush. Silas is not the informer.

## Chapter climax

At the final door Adrian refuses to let Noah charge armed guards; both surrender when escape would get the other shot. Rusk admits leaving the route, claiming he thought that was all they wanted. His daughter’s treatment leverage is confirmed in CH02; it does not excuse his choice.

## Ending transition

They hear one another through a solitary vent. Noah says “The harbor light”; Adrian answers “Still turns,” repeating the ambiguous cached recording rather than asserting Elena is alive. Mara receives a falsified injury report and requests infirmary transfer.

## Connection to next chapter

The failed route exposes the prison’s contractor connection and gives Mara a medical basis to move them; CH02 begins with that transfer.
