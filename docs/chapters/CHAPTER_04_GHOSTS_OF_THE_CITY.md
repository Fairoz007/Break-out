# CH04 — Ghosts of the City

Time: 19 October evening–20 October dawn. Canonical authority: [STORY.md](../../STORY.md).

## Opening situation

The pair enter a city where Adrian’s face remains on wanted bulletins and Noah’s dock contacts are watched.

## Major events

A retired archivist provides a lawful public access route to contract microfilm; restricted signature records require retrieving a sealed duplicate before VANTAGE destroys the annex. Cooperative observation and records handling reveal shell companies and a scheduled refrigerated shipment. The relay-query log proves Adrian’s unredacted verification request exposed Elena’s location. Adrian confesses before Noah reads the final page.

## Character development

Noah can express anger or reserve judgment; both responses remain valid. Adrian relinquishes control of the proof and his own account.

## Conflict

Pursuit threatens the archive staff; players must evacuate witnesses while carrying a finite evidence case.

## Important discoveries

Vale approved the opaque contract system, then tried to expose its use for detention. Adrian was framed because he could authenticate Vale’s corrective records.

## Plot twists

A morally compromised minister can still be a murder victim and useful witness through records. Adrian’s negligence is real, but Voss ordered the violence.

## Chapter climax

The pair preserve the shipment schedule as the annex burns, each relying on the other to exit.

## Ending transition

They learn Elena is aboard a container convoy leaving the port the next night.

## Connection to next chapter

The convoy schedule defines CH05’s rescue window; the evidence chain remains incomplete.
